**Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR)** lies to the south, about **35 km** from Nha Trang city centre. After your flight you have a few ways into town, each with its own pros and cons.

## 1. Public bus

Usually the cheapest option. On the other hand, it makes many stops, runs only during set hours and is awkward with a lot of luggage. Check the latest timetable before you fly, especially if you land in the evening.

## 2. Taxi and ride-hailing

Available right at the airport and straight to your hotel. Fast and flexible, but much more expensive than shared options, especially if you travel alone.

## 3. Pre-booked private transfer

A driver waits for you and takes you to the door. Great for families and groups. Prices are per vehicle, so it is costly for one or two people.

## 4. Budget airport shuttle like Origo

A middle ground: **from <strong class="price-hl">80,000 VND</strong> per person**, much cheaper than a taxi, a car within 30 minutes of landing, and drop-off at fixed points in Nha Trang centre. Before pickup you receive the driver name and plate number.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Lowest cost and no rush:** the bus.
- **Large group, door-to-door:** a private transfer or taxi.
- **Save money but stay fast and supported:** a budget shuttle like Origo.

## Tips for arrival

- Keep your flight number and landing time handy to share with your transport provider.
- Get a SIM or eSIM so you can be reached as soon as you land.
- Always check the plate number and driver name before getting in.
- Carry a little cash in VND for small expenses.

Prices and schedules change, so check before you travel.
