Just landed at **Cam Ranh International Airport** and wondering how to get into Nha Trang quickly, safely and without overspending? **Origo** offers shuttle tickets **from <strong class="price-hl">80,000 VND</strong> per person**.

## What is Origo?

Origo sells shuttle tickets from Cam Ranh Airport to Nha Trang city centre. You book online, our staff contact you to confirm, and a car is ready for you after landing.

## Why ride with Origo?

- **Affordable:** from <strong class="price-hl">80,000 VND</strong> per person, much cheaper than a regular taxi on the same route.
- **Fast:** rides run continuously, so you will have a car within 30 minutes of landing.
- **Reliable:** before pickup you receive the driver name and plate number. Origo confirms them and supports you throughout the trip.
- **Clear stops:** fixed points in Nha Trang city centre, with address and map.

## Drop-off points in Nha Trang

- **70 Trần Nhật Duật:** about 1.5 km from Trần Phú beach and 2 km from the city centre.
- **Vành Đai Road:** about 5 km from Trần Phú beach and 3 km from the city centre.



## How to book

**Booking:**

1. Book online on our website.
2. Our staff contact you.
3. Confirm pickup time, number of passengers and drop-off point.
4. Pay and receive your ticket code.

**Pickup:**

1. 15 minutes before pickup, we send the driver name, plate number and meeting area.
2. Check the car and driver.
3. Ride to your chosen drop-off point.
