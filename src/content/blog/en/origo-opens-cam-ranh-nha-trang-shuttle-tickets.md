**Origo** now takes bookings for a shuttle between **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR) and central Nha Trang**, built for solo travellers who want a quick, low-cost ride into the city.

## Key points

- **Ticket price:** from <strong class="price-hl">80,000 VND</strong> per person, one flat price, no vehicle choice.
- **One way:** Cam Ranh Airport → Nha Trang.
- **Quick pickup:** a ride is ready 30 minutes after your plane lands.
- **Clear drop-off points:** two at first, the Origo office (85TT3-K98 Tran Nhat Duat) and 19 Nguyen Duc Canh (South Nha Trang). More will be added as details are confirmed.
- **Know your driver:** 15 minutes before pickup we send the driver's name, plate number and meeting area.

## How to book

Enter your name, phone number, travel date and time, and direction in the booking form on the home page. An Origo agent will contact you to advise and confirm, then you pay and receive your ticket code.

## What's next

Origo will post new pickup and drop-off points and service changes in this section. For other ways to get from the airport, read [Cam Ranh Airport to Nha Trang: 4 ways to get there](/en/nha-trang-guide/cam-ranh-airport-to-nha-trang/).
